CoinCover Control is where your organisation manages its backups, decides who can approve a recovery, and runs recoveries when a key is lost or compromised. It's non-custodial by design: nobody outside the approvers you name — not an administrator, and not CoinCover — can release your material.

This guide takes a new user from an invitation through to their first sign-in. There are two roles. **Organisation Owners** manage the team and set up who can approve recoveries. **Members** approve recoveries, and vote on changes to the backups they're responsible for.

## Before you start

You need an invitation — you can't self-register. An Organisation Owner (or CoinCover) invites you by email.

## How your organisation signs in

CoinCover sets up sign-in for each organisation. There are three parts to it: how users sign in, which second factor they use, and whether sensitive actions need a hardware security key.

### Sign-in method

| Method | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| **Password** | Users sign in with their email address and a password. This is the default. |
| **Magic link** | Users enter their email address and we send them a one-time sign-in link. The link expires after 15 minutes and works once. Password sign-in is turned off. |
| **Password and magic link** | Users choose either option on the sign-in page. |
| **Single sign-on (SSO)** | Users sign in through your identity provider. You can make SSO the only way in, or keep passwords as a fallback. |

SSO uses OpenID Connect (OIDC) and is set up for Okta. If you use a different OIDC identity provider, talk to us. SAML isn't supported, and magic links can't be combined with SSO.

<Accordion title="What to know before setting up SSO">
  - **You send us** your identity provider's issuer URL, plus the client ID and client secret of the app you create for Control.
  - **We send you** the redirect URI to add to that app. We can also give you a sign-in URL if you want a Control tile on your Okta dashboard.
  - **People still need an invitation.** SSO doesn't create Control accounts on its own. Someone can sign in with SSO only after they've been invited to Control.
  - **Roles are managed in Control.** Groups and claims in your identity provider aren't mapped to Control roles.
  - **Your identity provider must send a verified email address.** It has to match the email address the person was invited with.
  - **MFA happens in your identity provider.** Control doesn't ask SSO users for a second factor at sign-in, so enforce MFA in your identity provider. A security key requirement for sensitive actions (below) still applies.
</Accordion>

### Two-factor authentication

Every user who signs in with a password or magic link sets up a second factor the first time they sign in. Two types are available:

| Second factor | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| **Authenticator app** | A 6-digit code from an app such as Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, Authy or 1Password. |
| **Security key** | A hardware security key such as a YubiKey, or a passkey stored on the user's device. |

By default, users choose either one. We can limit your organisation to authenticator apps only, or to security keys only. A change applies to new set-ups; methods that users have already set up keep working. SMS codes aren't supported.

### Security keys for sensitive actions

We can also require your users to confirm sensitive actions with a security key, such as voting on a recovery request or on a change to an Access Control List. Users register their keys the first time they sign in after this is turned on. See [Set up your security keys](/guides/control/security-keys).

<Tip>
  To change how your organisation signs in, set up SSO, or require security keys, contact your CoinCover account manager or [support@coincover.com](mailto:support@coincover.com).
</Tip>

## Set up your account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Accept your invitation">
    You'll receive an invitation email from CoinCover. Open it and select **Get started** to open the sign-up page. Use the same email address the invitation was sent to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Enter your name and choose a password. Passwords need at least 8 characters, and must include an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a number, and a special character.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your email">
    CoinCover sends a verification code to your email address. Enter it to confirm your account. If it doesn't arrive, request a new code and check your spam folder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up two-factor authentication">
    Choose how you'll confirm it's you each time you sign in. You only see the options your organisation allows.

    - **Authenticator app:** scan the on-screen QR code with your authenticator app, or enter the key manually, then enter the 6-digit code it generates.
    - **Security key:** insert your key and tap it when your browser asks, or use a passkey on your device. See [Set up your security keys](/guides/control/security-keys).

    <Warning>
      Keep your second factor safe. If you use security keys, register a backup key as well. If you lose access to every method you've set up, contact CoinCover support to regain access to your account.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete identity verification">
    On your first sign-in you complete an identity check. It runs in your browser and takes a few minutes. Identity verification protects every sensitive action in Control, so it has to be completed before you can use the platform.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register your security keys, if asked">
    If your organisation requires security keys for sensitive actions, Control asks you to register them before you continue. By default, that's two keys: one you use day to day and a backup. See [Set up your security keys](/guides/control/security-keys).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **If your organisation uses SSO**, you don't create a password, verify your email with a code, or set up a second factor in Control. Enter the email address your invitation was sent to, select **Continue with Okta**, and follow your provider's prompts. You still complete identity verification, and register security keys if your organisation requires them.
</Note>

## Signing in

Enter your email address first. Control then shows the sign-in options your organisation uses: **Sign in** with a password, **Send magic link**, or **Continue with Okta**. After a password or magic link, confirm it's you with your authenticator app or security key. If you've set up both, you can choose either.

## Finding your way around

Once you're in, you land on the **Backups** page, which lists every backup your organisation holds. The sidebar has four areas:

| Area | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Backups** | Every backup package your organisation holds. This is the default landing page. |
| **Access Control Lists** | Who can approve the recovery of each backup, and any changes to those lists. |
| **Recoveries** | Every recovery, in progress and past. |
| **Team** | The people in your organisation. Owners invite and remove users here. |

The bell at the top right shows anything waiting on you — a recovery to approve, or a change to an Access Control List you're on.
