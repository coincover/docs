<Update label="Sept 2026 · v1.23.10">
  **New features**

  - **Recovery Readiness certificates** — Once a readiness programme is complete, owners can issue and download a signed certificate as a durable record of your readiness position, ready to share with auditors and stakeholders.
  - **Policy review in the browser** — Recovery policies can now be read and reviewed without leaving Control. The policy document opens in a built-in viewer, and reviewers can leave comments against it and reply in threaded discussions. Owners are notified when a policy is waiting on them, and draft comments stay private to the reviewer until published.
  - **Support-assisted multi-factor reset** — If you lose access to your authenticator app or security key, support can now take you through a verified reset to restore access to your account.
  - **Signer set administration** — Administrators can create and manage signer sets used for partner verification, including adding and removing signers and reviewing the current membership of each set.
  - **Environment indicator** — A persistent badge shows which organisation and environment you are working in, making it immediately clear whether you are acting against test or live data.
  - **Move backups between partner organisations** — Backups can now be transferred between partner organisations, and customers operating several workspaces under a single recovery arrangement are now handled correctly.
  - **Streamlined invitation links** — New team members joining via an invitation link now land on a dedicated sign-up page and confirm their invite with a one-time code.

  **Improvements**

  - Invitations that have expired or already been used now explain what happened and offer a route to request a new one, instead of failing without context.
  - Verification codes now use a slotted input, making codes easier to enter and to paste.
  - Added a dedicated screen for setting a new password when one is required at sign-in.
  - Recovery Readiness training is now delivered through an in-app course player, so assigned training can be completed without leaving Control.
  - Reduced first-response delays when signing in.
  - Notifications now appear at the bottom of the screen, where they are less likely to obscure page content.
  - Refreshed tables throughout the product with clearer loading states, consistent borders, and tidier row actions.
  - Empty states across the recovery lists are clearer about when there is genuinely nothing to show.
  - In-app feedback is now routed directly to the product support team.
  - Partner badges now appear alongside identity information, and adapt automatically to light or dark mode.
  - Access control lists now validate quorum and vetoer/mandatory member configurations up front, preventing invalid setups from being saved.

  **Bug fixes**

  - Fixed an issue where syncing could fail on workspaces with a large number of vaults. Synchronisation now completes reliably on these accounts.
  - Fixed an issue where signing in with an email sign-in link could fail for accounts that use an authenticator app, even when a valid code was entered.
  - Fixed an issue where identity verification could be treated as incomplete after you had finished it, leaving you unable to progress.
  - Fixed an issue where backup details would not display when no access control list had been assigned to the backup.
  - Fixed an issue where members of a backup's access control list could be missing from the details view.
  - Fixed an issue where searching a table that returned no matches left the search box unusable until the page was reloaded.
  - Fixed an issue where a cold recovery could fail to start when none of the available packages supported key recovery.
  - Fixed an issue where an access control list set up with only one or two members could become stuck and unable to progress.
  - Fixed an issue preventing legacy backups from being soft-deleted.

  **Security**

  - Multi-factor verification is now applied consistently when signing in with an email sign-in link, matching the behaviour of password sign-in. Accounts with an authenticator app or security key enrolled are always prompted to verify before access is granted.
  - Hardened the multi-factor reset and recovery process.
  - Recovery policy activity is now forwarded to our central security monitoring pipeline.
</Update>

<Update label="Sept 2026 · v1.21.0">
  **New features**

  - **Invitation-only signup** — Signing up to Control is now restricted to people who have received an invitation from an administrator, or who are completing an administrator-initiated reset. Nobody can self-register against your organisation.
  - **Access control lists** — Access control lists are now first-class objects you can manage in their own right, rather than being tied to a single backup. New list and detail pages show every list, who is on it, and which backups it governs — and a single list can now cover multiple backups. Approval requests link straight to the list they affect, so approvers can review a change in context.
  - **Recovery policy review and attestation** — Recovery policies now go through a documented review and attestation step. Policies carry version history and are superseded cleanly when replaced, and member tasks and Test Recovery unlock once the policy has been attested.
  - **Test Recovery drills** — Owners can run a Test Recovery drill end to end, including a one-time download, to prove a recovery would work before it is ever needed. Control now checks the required prerequisites are in place before a drill can start, so you find gaps up front instead of mid-drill.
  - **Recovery Readiness training** — Readiness training can now be assigned and tracked for the people named in your recovery plan, with progress visible alongside their other readiness tasks.
  - **Resend pending invitations** — You can now resend an invitation to any user who hasn't yet completed signup, straight from the system users list. Previously the only option was to remove the user and invite them again.
  - **Security keys for approvals** — The security key you register for sign-in now also works for organisation-level approval prompts, so you can use one method throughout.

  **Bug fixes**

  - Fixed an error when creating an API key that prevented some administrators from completing the action.
  - Pending access control change requests are now only shown to genuine members of the affected list. Previously they could also appear for users who held broad read permissions but no membership.
  - Approval notifications for an access control change now reach every member of the relevant list, including where that list covers several backups.
  - A recovery can no longer be started while a change to the governing access control list is still awaiting approval.
  - Fixed several issues on the access control screens: the total count no longer changes when you filter, the backups table refreshes immediately after you assign a list, and you can now undo the outgoing side of a pending member replacement.
  - Fixed issues in the Recovery Readiness setup flow, where some steps were unclear and certain capabilities were incorrectly shown as unavailable to eligible organisations.
  - Uploading a recovery policy now works reliably from Control.
  - The organisation switcher now names the organisation you are currently working in.

  **Security**

  - Applied the outstanding dependency advisories across the platform. No customer action is required.
</Update>

<Update label="Aug 2026 · v1.20">
  **New features**

  - **Sign in with a YubiKey** — You can now use a YubiKey or other WebAuthn security key as your multi-factor method at sign-in, as an alternative to an authenticator app. You choose which method to use when you are prompted.
  - **Organisation API keys** — Organisations can now create and manage their own API keys, so you can integrate Control with your own systems.
  - **Recovery Readiness (early access)** — First release of Recovery Readiness. Owners can begin setting up their readiness programme and assign tasks to members. Availability is controlled per organisation — speak to your account contact if you would like it enabled.

  **Improvements**

  - The vault list is now paginated and usable on accounts with a large number of vaults.

  **Bug fixes**

  - Vault sync is significantly more reliable. Syncs no longer get stuck in a pending state on large accounts, no longer fail part-way through when a custody provider connection expires, and correctly resume on a first-time sync. Large vault sets now sync completely rather than stopping short.
  - Fixed an issue where preparing a hot-key-only recovery download would fail.
  - Fixed an issue where members could not complete multi-factor setup if sign-in MFA was not enabled for their organisation.
  - You can now remove a partner from an organisation when it has no backups assigned to it.
  - Clearer error messages when assigning a user to an organisation or sending an invitation fails.

  **Security**

  - Applied the outstanding high and medium severity dependency advisories across the platform. No customer action is required.
</Update>
